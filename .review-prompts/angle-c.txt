## Conventions
- Commits: Conventional Commits, imperative mood. No Co-Authored-By trailer.
- Search: use `fd` for files, `rg` for text — never `find`/`grep`.
- Errors: fail fast, never swallow exceptions silently.
- Verification: proving something works means exercising it for real, not
  just confirming it's listed or covered by a mock.
